Terms of use · Effective November 12, 2026

Personal household use

School Board MagicMirror is a personal household project for organizing school notices and events. It is not a school-operated service, an official school record, or a public subscription product. There is no charge for household use of this app.

Account authorization

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to manage. A parent or guardian must manage authorization for supervised accounts. Google permissions are granted through Google sign-in and can be revoked through Google Account connections or Family Link. See the privacy notice for how data is used and stored.

Check important details

Notices may arrive late, be incomplete, or be interpreted incorrectly. Check the original email, calendar, or school announcement for important dates, cancellations, deadlines, and instructions. The display does not replace communication with the school.

Availability and control

This household project is provided as available, with no promised uptime or support schedule. Google access, network access, and source feeds may stop working. The household administrator controls setup, updates, removal of locally stored information, and where the display can be seen.

Contact and updates

Household members can contact their administrator directly. General technical questions can be raised on the public project support page; never include private emails, account credentials, or children's information there. This page will be updated when the app's use changes.
