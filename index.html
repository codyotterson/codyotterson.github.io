School Board MagicMirror brings school notices and upcoming school events onto a household display.

How it works

The account owner connects selected Gmail accounts through Google sign-in. The app uses read-only Gmail access to identify school messages and prepare a brief overview for the family's local MagicMirror. Calendar feeds can provide upcoming school events.

This is a personal household app, not a public sign-up service. This website contains app information only; it does not display messages, schedules, account credentials, or children's information.

Your account stays under your control

The app requests permission to view email messages and settings. It cannot send mail, change messages, or delete them. Access can be removed through your Google Account or, for supervised accounts, Family Link.
