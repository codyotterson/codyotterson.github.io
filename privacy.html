Effective November 12, 2026. This notice covers the personal household School Board MagicMirror app and this informational website.

Google data and its purpose

When an account owner authorizes the app, it receives an account identifier and OAuth credentials. Read-only Gmail access allows it to retrieve email metadata and message content to identify school notices, dates, reminders, and updates for a local household display. This permission covers the authorized mailbox; school-only filtering is performed by the app and is not a Google-enforced restriction on the permission.

The app does not request permission to send, edit, or delete email. Calendar feeds configured by the household may also supply school event details.

Storage and disclosure

OAuth credentials are stored in a private account directory on the household's mirror computer, separately from publicly served display files. School summaries and any local processing records remain on household-controlled devices. The display is visible to people who can see the household screen.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website. The app does not send Google message data to third-party AI providers. Any future use of such a provider requires an updated disclosure and the account owner's approval before enabling it. Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Use and transfer of Google API information will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Authorization credentials are retained until the household removes the connection or Google revokes it. Locally stored summaries and processing records can be removed by the household administrator; this does not delete source Gmail messages. Disconnecting a Google account does not automatically erase previously stored local summaries.

To stop access, revoke School Board MagicMirror in your Google Account connections. For supervised accounts, a parent can manage access through Family Link. Ask the household administrator to remove local credentials and records as well.

Children's accounts and changes

This app is used by a household for its own school planning. A parent or guardian manages authorization for supervised accounts and controls what appears on the family display. The app is not offered as a public service to children.

This notice will be updated before material changes to how Google data is used or shared.

This public website

This website has no forms, advertising, analytics scripts, or application sign-in. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor IP addresses and request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Gmail data and credentials are not stored in its repository.

Contact the maintainer through the project support page. Public requests should contain no private account data; household members can contact their administrator directly.
